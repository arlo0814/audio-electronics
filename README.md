# audio-electronics
An Analog Electronics Project aimed to construct a 10-band equalizer, a Class AB Amplifier, with a dedicated Power Supply Board

In Partial Fulfillment to the Course Electronics 1 and Electronics 2

## Power Supply Board
The power supply board consists of a `18-0-18 V` transformer rated at `4 Amperes`. The rectifier bridge is composed on four `10a10` diodes, which is rated at `10 Ampere` at `1000 volts peak`.
The filter capacitors are two `4700 uF` rated at `50 Volts`.
