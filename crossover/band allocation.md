# Band Allocation
$$f_c = \frac{R}{2\pi L} = \frac{8\ \Omega}{2\pi \cdot 1.2\ \text{mH}} = 1.061\ \text{kHz}$$
$$f_o = \frac{1}{2\pi \sqrt{LC}} = \frac{1}{2\pi \sqrt{(0.6\ \text{mH})(6.8\ \mu\text{F})}} = 2.492\ \text{kHz}$$
$$f_c = \frac{1}{2\pi RC} = \frac{1}{2\pi \cdot (3.74 + 8)\Omega \cdot (1.5 + 1.5)\mu\text{F}} = 4.519\ \text{kHz}$$
